Third-party components bundled with ASUS Fan Controller
=======================================================

AsusFanControl.exe below is bundled in src/asusfancontrol/assets and embedded
in the built AsusFanControlUI.exe. AsusWinIO64.dll is NOT bundled; it is copied
from the local ASUS driver store at runtime. Each remains under its owner's terms.
