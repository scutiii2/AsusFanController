Third-party components used by ASUS Fan Controller
==================================================

Nothing third-party is bundled in the built AsusFanControlUI.exe apart from the
Python and Qt libraries PyInstaller packages (PySide6/Qt under LGPLv3,
pyqtgraph under MIT, and their dependencies, each under its owner's terms).


AsusFanControl (credit only - not bundled)
------------------------------------------
https://github.com/Karmel0x/AsusFanControl (MIT License, (c) 2023 Karmel0x).

Its code and binary are not included in this software.


AsusWinIO64.dll (not bundled - used at runtime)
-----------------------------------------------
(c) ASUSTeK COMPUTER INC. Not redistributed with this software. The MyASUS app
(ASUS System Control Interface) installs it under
C:\Windows\System32\DriverStore\FileRepository\asussci2.inf_amd64_*\ASUSSystemAnalysis\
and the app copies it from there at startup.
